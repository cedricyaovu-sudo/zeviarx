Notice of Privacy Practices

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed and how you can get access to this information. Please review it carefully.

1. Who this notice covers

This Notice of Privacy Practices (“Notice”) is issued by Zevia Rx, Inc. (“Zevia Rx,” “we”) and applies to the protected health information (“PHI”) we create, receive, maintain or transmit when you use the Zevia Rx platform. The independent, licensed healthcare providers who review your intake through MD Integrations (MDI), and Hallandale Pharmacy, which dispenses any prescribed medication, are separate covered entities under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (“HIPAA”). Each maintains its own medical records and its own notice of privacy practices; we coordinate with them so that your requests under this Notice reach the right record holder.

2. What protected health information we hold

PHI we hold includes the health intake you complete (height, weight, medical history, pregnancy status, allergies, current medications and conditions), your date of birth and sex, identity documents and verification videos you upload, your delivery address and contact details, the treatment you requested, and the outcome and status of your consultation, prescription and shipments. Payment card details are processed by our payment processors and are never stored by Zevia Rx.

3. How we may use and disclose your PHI

Treatment. We transmit your intake, identity verification and health information to the licensed provider assigned to your case so they can evaluate you, and to the dispensing pharmacy so a prescription, if issued, can be filled and shipped to you.

Payment. We use the minimum information necessary to process your payment, financing application and any refund under our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Health care operations. We use PHI to operate, secure and improve the platform, to provide customer support, to respond to your questions, and to meet our legal and quality obligations.

As required or permitted by law. We may disclose PHI when required by law, to public-health authorities, to report adverse drug events to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration, to comply with a court order or lawful subpoena, or to prevent a serious threat to health or safety.

Business associates. Service providers that host data, deliver email or text messages, or support operations on our behalf may receive PHI only under written agreements that require them to safeguard it.

Uses that require your written authorization. We do not sell PHI. We do not use or disclose PHI for marketing by third parties, and we do not use your health information for advertising. Any other use not described in this Notice requires your written authorization, which you may revoke at any time in writing.

4. How your records are stored and protected

Electronic records are encrypted in transit and at rest and stored in access-controlled systems located in the United States. Identity documents and verification videos are kept in a private, non-public repository; access is limited to the systems and personnel needed to deliver your care and to the licensed provider reviewing your case. We maintain audit logging and retain records only for as long as required to provide care and meet legal retention obligations.

5. Your rights regarding your PHI

  • Access and copies of your records. You may request a copy of the health information we hold about you, including your intake and consultation outcome, in electronic or paper form. We respond within 30 days of a verified request. Records created by your provider (clinical notes, the prescription itself) are held by MDI, and dispensing records by Hallandale Pharmacy; on request we will forward your request to them and help you obtain those records.
  • Amendment. You may ask us to correct information you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Accounting of disclosures. You may request a list of certain disclosures we have made of your PHI in the six years before your request.
  • Restrictions. You may ask us to limit how we use or share your PHI. We are not always required to agree, but we will honor a request not to share information with a health plan about services you paid for in full out of pocket.
  • Confidential communications. You may ask us to contact you in a specific way or at a specific address, and we will accommodate reasonable requests.
  • Breach notification. We will notify you if a breach occurs that compromises the privacy or security of your PHI.
  • A paper copy of this Notice. Available on request at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@zeviarx.com with the subject line “Privacy request” or write to the address below. We will verify your identity before releasing records.

6. Complaints

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may file a complaint with us at support@zeviarx.com or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights (hhs.gov/ocr/complaints). We will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint.

7. Changes to this Notice

We may update this Notice; the current version is always posted at this address with its effective date. Material changes apply to all PHI we maintain.
