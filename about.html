About Zevia Rx

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Zevia Rx exists to make doctor-prescribed GLP-1 weight loss care simple, transparent, and accessible — without insurance paperwork, waiting rooms, or hidden pricing.

What we do

Zevia Rx is a telehealth platform. We built the front door: a structured health intake you complete in about two minutes, transparent plans with pricing shown before you pay, and logistics that bring care to you. Behind that door, the medicine is practiced by the people the law entrusts it to — independent, licensed clinicians in your state review every intake and make every medical decision, and state-licensed US pharmacies prepare and ship any medication that is prescribed, in temperature-controlled, cold-chain packaging.

We are not a pharmacy and not a medical group. We do not manufacture, dispense, or ship medication, and we never decide who is prescribed — a prescription is never guaranteed, and purchasing a plan does not create one. If your provider determines treatment is not appropriate, your medication cost and shipping fee are refunded under our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

What we believe

  • Price transparency. The full cost of your plan — medication, provider consultation, and shipping — is itemized on one screen before you pay. No insurance required, no surprise bills.
  • Provider-led care. Every intake is reviewed by a licensed clinician in your state. Clinical judgment, not marketing, decides who is treated.
  • Straight talk about medication. Where compounded medication is prescribed, it is prepared by state-licensed compounding pharmacies and is not FDA-approved; we say so plainly, everywhere it matters.
  • Privacy by default. Health information is encrypted, access-controlled, never sold, and never used for third-party advertising. See our Privacy Policy.

Where we are in our journey

Zevia Rx is a new company preparing for launch. Our licensed care team and pharmacy partners will be announced and verifiable on this page before care begins — we would rather show you real clinicians than borrowed stock photos.
