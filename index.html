Questions

Everything you're wondering.

What exactly is compounded GLP-1 medication?

Compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are prepared by state-licensed US compounding pharmacies using the same active ingredient class as brand-name GLP-1s. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved, but the pharmacies that prepare them are state-regulated. Your provider will determine whether a compounded option is appropriate for you.

Do I need insurance?

No. Zevia Rx is priced for cash pay — $125 for your first month, then a price-locked $299/mo. No insurance paperwork, no prior authorizations, no surprise bills.

How fast will I get my medication?

Most members complete the quiz in about 2 minutes. If a licensed provider approves your prescription, your first shipment typically arrives within days in cold-chain packaging.

What if I'm declined for a payment plan?

No problem — you can always start month-to-month with a standard credit or debit card at $125 for your first month. You'll see every option at checkout.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Month-to-month plans can be paused or cancelled anytime from your member portal. Multi-month programs run for their committed term, which is how we keep the pricing low.

Is this safe?

GLP-1 medications are prescription-only for a reason. That's why every Zevia Rx plan starts with a health intake reviewed by a licensed clinician in your state, who determines eligibility and appropriate dosing, with ongoing check-ins included.
